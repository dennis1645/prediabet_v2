# Resep: Bubur sagu

**Kode:** BO023
**Nama Asli Resep:** Bubur Sagu Ambon
**Kategori Bahan:** Umbi-umbian, Bumbu Olahan, Seafood, Santan

## Bahan-bahan

- 4 keping Sagu Ambon
- 1 buah Ubi oranye, potong dadu
- 0.5 keping Gula jawa
- 100 gr Gula pasir
- 0.25 sdt Vanila esens
- 1 lembar Daun pandan, ikat simpul
- Air
- 65 ml Santan Rose Brand
- 500 ml Air
- 0.5 sdt Garam
