# Resep: Tepung Ubi Ungu

**Kode:** BO072
**Nama Asli Resep:** Pasta Ubi Ungu (Isian Bakpao/Roti)
**Kategori Bahan:** Umbi-umbian, Santan, Susu, Bumbu Olahan, Biji-bijian

## Bahan-bahan

- 596 gr ubi ungu
- 120 ml air untuk merebus ubi ungu
- 65 ml santan instan Rose Brand
- 58 gr kuah santan susu
- 0.25 sdt vanilla butter
- 30 gr gula pasir Rose Brand
- 20 gr tepung ketan putih
- 10 gr Evoo
- 1 gr himalayan salt
- 45 gr air mineral
